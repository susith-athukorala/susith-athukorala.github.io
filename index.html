About

Clinical experience with a digital health focus

I am a medical doctor and health informatician with specialist postgraduate training in biomedical informatics and health informatics. I am a Fellow of the Australasian Institute of Digital Health (FAIDH) and a Certified Health Informatician Australasia (CHIA). My work spans clinical care, healthcare information systems, interoperability, digital health strategy, academic teaching and research.

I have contributed to public-sector healthcare, cancer care, telemedicine, health information management and postgraduate education, with a strong interest in making digital systems clinically meaningful, safe and practical for real-world use.
